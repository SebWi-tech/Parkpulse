# ParkPulse — Competitor Analysis

## Scope and method
Research focus: Germany.
Method: desk research using provider product pages,
pricing pages, help pages and published documentation.

This is not a hands-on test of all products.
Features may vary by country, facility and tariff.

## Competitor profiles
### EasyPark
### APCOA FLOW
### Q-Park
### Parkopedia
### Parkster
### ParkingList
### Wemolo

## Comparison dimensions
- Parking discovery
- Availability information
- Payment
- Reservations
- Discounts and loyalty
- EV charging
- Operator integration
- Geographic and facility limitations

## Implications for ParkPulse
- Do not claim that parking payments or discounts are new.
- Focus on a clear shopping-benefit and rewards journey.
- Validate reward funding with potential partners.
- Distinguish the prototype from production integrations.

## Evidence limitations
"Not established in the reviewed sources" does not mean
that a competitor cannot offer a feature.
